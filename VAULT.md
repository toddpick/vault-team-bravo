# The Two-Key Vault

The shared vault in the lobby opens with a **6-digit code**.

- **Digits 1–3: Team Alpha's half.**
- **Digits 4–6: Team Bravo's half** (that's us).

## Our half (Team Bravo)

Our half is **the number of the floor tile Team Alpha painted gold** for the lobby mural.

We weren't part of the mural project, so we don't know which tile that is. Team Alpha does.

## Their half (Team Alpha)

Team Alpha set their own half. Ask them.

## What's inside

Sealed until the vault is open.
